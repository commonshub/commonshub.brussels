Thank you for becoming a member of the Commons Hub. Memberships are how we pay the rent and keep this common space open for everyone. We're glad you're with us.

YOUR FIRST STEPS

1. Get Discord. Discord is where the community talks, plans and asks for help. Get the app for your phone or your computer (it's free).
https://discord.com/download

2. Join us on Discord. Join our server, then reply to this email with your Discord username: we'll give you the member role, which opens the members' channels and the door.
https://discord.commonshub.brussels

3. Open the door. With the member role, go to the #door channel and type "open": the door at Rue de la Madeleine 51 buzzes open within a few seconds. Tip: add the #door channel to your favourites.
https://discord.com/channels/1280532848604086365/1306678821751230514

4. Come to the Heartbeat, if you like. Every Monday, 13:00–14:00 at the hub: what's happening, what's needed, who does what. It's optional, and a nice way to meet everyone.
https://discord.com/channels/1280532848604086365/1443604524307583099

5. Read the handbook. How the hub works: the door, the kitchen, the rooms, the tokens.
https://commonshub.brussels/handbook

6. Take a first shift. Welcome people at an event and earn tokens: the quickest way to meet everyone. Pick one on commonshub.brussels/shifts, or in the #shifts channel.
https://discord.com/channels/1280532848604086365/1443604838255427584

WHAT YOUR MEMBERSHIP GIVES YOU
- Take part in decisions and help shape the Hub: make proposals, and earn tokens for the time you give.
- Help care for the space and our shared resources.
- Lower prices when you book rooms or events.
- The Friday open coworking for members, newcomers and prospective commoners, and our community activities.
- The members' channels on Discord and, if you like, the weekly Heartbeat meeting (Mondays, 13:00–14:00).

Coworking on other days is an extra service for members, charged separately: €25 a day or €100 a month (+ VAT), or in tokens, 2 a day or 1 for half a day (type /buy on Discord).

Questions? Ask Elinor, our community bot, on Discord (https://elinor.commonshub.brussels), or just reply to this email.

See you at the hub!
